FAQ

The questions everyone asks first

Who owns the website when it's done?

You do — the domain, the code, the content and every login. Nothing is held hostage. If you ever want to move to another developer, you can, and we'll help with the handoff.

How long does it take?

A Starter Site typically launches in 1–2 weeks and a Business Site in 2–4, counting from the day we have your content. We've turned sites around in 7 days when there was a deadline — tell us if you have one.

Do I have to write all the content?

No. We draft the copy from a short interview and your existing materials, then you edit and approve. If you already have text you like, we'll work with it.

Where are you based? Do you work with US clients?

We're a small studio based in Mexico, working Central Time year round, and we work with clients across the US, Canada and Mexico. In practice that means a six-hour-plus overlap with your workday, calls in English, and answers the same day rather than the next morning.

What exactly does the AI assistant do?

It's trained on your services, hours, pricing and the questions you get asked constantly. It answers visitors on your site and on WhatsApp, in English or Spanish, collects the details you need to quote, and hands the conversation to you when it should. On our call we'll walk you through a live one, set up for a business like yours.

What happens after launch? Am I locked into a contract?

Small edits are free for the first 30 days. After that, hosting and maintenance are month to month and you can cancel any time — or take the site and host it yourself. The AI assistant subscription works the same way.

How do payments work?

Half up front, half at launch, in US dollars. Card, bank transfer, PayPal or Wise. You get an invoice for everything.
